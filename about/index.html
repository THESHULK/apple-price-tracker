About

Apple Price Tracker checks the prices of popular Apple products every day across major US retailers — Best Buy, Target, Walmart, B&H Photo, Costco, Amazon, Adorama, and Abt. Each product gets a price history chart so you can see what it really costs over time, and our deals page only lists products currently selling below their 90-day low. No inflated list prices, no fake urgency — just the data you need to know whether today's price is actually a good one.

Prices and availability change frequently. We do our best to keep data fresh, but the retailer's own page is always the final source of truth before you buy.
